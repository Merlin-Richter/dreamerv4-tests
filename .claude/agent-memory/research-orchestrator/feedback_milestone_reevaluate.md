At milestones (hypothesis resolved, phase boundary), Merlin wants a genuine
reevaluation of the trajectory and the task list — not mechanical continuation of
the pre-existing backlog (T-002 → T-004 → …). When he calls a milestone, bring a
critical re-read: are the queued tasks still the right ones? what's cheaper/more
decisive? what alternatives exist? Recommend, then seek alignment before building.

**Why:** At the 2026-06-12 H1-closure milestone he answered three design questions
but added "we still need to discuss the trajectory and what the next tasks should
be. Don't blindly continue with the tasks. We need to reevaluate also." Answering
structured forks is not the same as him approving the plan.

**How to apply:** After a milestone fork is answered, do NOT jump into implementing
the backlog. Present a reevaluated trajectory (with alternatives + recommendation)
and get his buy-in first. Relates to [[feedback-measurement-validity]].
