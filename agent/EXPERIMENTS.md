| V-cache-equiv | Is the KV-cached rollout (generate) bit-exact to an uncached recompute-current-window reference, and does eviction break it? Root cause PINNED by depth discriminator: depth=3 (single temporal layer) stays mathematically exact through eviction (fp32 residual <=7.4e-4, collapses to ~3e-7 in fp64); depth>=6 (>=2 stacked temporal layers) diverges O(1) and does NOT shrink in fp64 (1.41 fp32==fp64) -> genuine, from frozen deep-temporal K/V encoding the commit-time receptive field. CORRECTION: divergence does NOT grow monotonically with temporal-layer count (d6~d9, sometimes d6>d9) on random weights; clean dichotomy is 1 vs >=2 temporal layers, not a graded depth effect.
